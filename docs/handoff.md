## What this is

Phone-first browser DAW proof of concept. SA3 (Stable Audio 3) generates a sample or an automatically tuned instrument on-device; a piano roll plays it chromatically. Design: `docs/design.md`. Project rules: `AGENTS.md`. The layout follows the PocketDaw mockups (`/home/deck/SketchBook/Projects/PocketDaw/docs/mockups/`); the user said not to change it. Style tokens come from AstraCamillaGui (`/home/deck/SketchBook/Projects/AstraMoode/AstraCamillaGui/client`).

Confirmed decisions: two generation paths, Sample (one-shot sampler) and Instrument (single-note hybrid resynthesis with automatic tuning); one looping pattern per track; single generation auto-kept; hand-drawn melodies only; models Small SFX, Small Music and Medium, each installed with one Download button into Cache Storage and removable (per model, all models, all app data); LAN self-signed HTTPS hosting from the Deck; mixer later.

## Playback performance with many tracks — 2026-10-11

- Measured (Chromium 1243 headless on the Steam Deck; `scripts/perf-probe`, 40-partial instruments, 16 notes per bar per track, 120 bpm). Offline render cost relative to real time (1.0 = just keeps up): 5 instrument tracks 0.20, 8 tracks 0.30, 10 tracks 0.38; 10 tracks with 5 instruments plus EQ and Hall reverb on each 0.39; sample tracks only, 10 tracks with EQ and Hall reverb on each 0.30. Time from Play to the first note in the real app (12 pitches per track): 5 tracks 0.7 s, 10 tracks 1.2 s; 10 tracks with 24 pitches 2.0 s, with 680 notes started in 10 s and none late.
- Voice cost: `voice.ts` advances partials by complex rotation (equal to the per-sample definition within 1e-4, `voice.test.ts`) instead of a cosine per partial per sample. `prepare.ts` rotates the sinc and window terms and caches the residual's forward FFT. `client.ts` prepares on up to three workers, sends each profile to a worker once (later requests carry a key and the pitch) and receives results by transfer.
- Main thread with 10 tracks: 3 % script time, heartbeat p99 below 3 ms, requestAnimationFrame p95 17 ms; 80 to 150 ms long tasks occur once around Play. One shared 40 ms timer drives all visible meters and only re-renders on a changed reading; the meter worklet stops posting while its input is silent; EqGraph reuses its canvas between frames.
- Scheduling: look-ahead is 0.3 s for the pattern scheduler and song playback. A note delivered late (main-thread stall or preparation) plays at once and keeps its original end; one more than 0.25 s late, or with less than 30 ms left, is skipped. Skips are summed per second of audio time into one `skipped stale notes` warning with a count and the largest lateness.
- Instrument voices per track (`processor.ts`): at most 8 sounding, the oldest is released when a ninth starts; at most 16 rendering (sounding plus releasing), the oldest is faded out over 10 ms; at most 64 queued future notes, the oldest queued note is dropped before it starts. Queued future notes are not counted as sounding. All instrument processors of a context share an oscillator budget of 256 (`voiceBudget.ts`): a new voice keeps its strongest `floor(256 / (active + 1))` partials, at least 12, and all partials while four or fewer voices sound.
- Prepared pitches (`assetCache.ts`, `player.ts`, `engine.ts`): a player installs up to `assetLimit` pitches (24 to 96 by residual size, 32 MB budget); the pitches the track's notes use are retained and only evicted beyond `hardAssetLimit` (24 to 128, 96 MB budget: 68 pitches for an 8 s source). `engine.play` retains and prepares them all; `setProject` updates the retained set and, while playing, prepares newly used pitches one at a time. A note whose pitch is not ready when due plays from the nearest installed pitch within 2 semitones (attack and residual of that pitch, partials at the note's pitch) and logs `note uses nearby pitch assets`; with no neighbour it waits and logs `late note preparation`.
- Why pitches are retained: preparing one pitch costs 66 ms of CPU on the Deck for an 8 s source (`prepareCost.mjs`; 15 ms for 2 s), and a phone core is several times slower. A pattern with more distinct pitches than a player keeps installed would re-prepare pitches every loop, start notes late and starve the audio thread (stalls). The phone report of choppy, clicking playback with one melody (2026-10-13) matches this; the phone itself is not measured.

## Chop (sound trim) — 2026-10-11

- Each sound has Start and End handles (Chop) on its waveform that keep a region of its original source; the source is stored whole, so widening restores it. Track field `trim` {start,end} seconds, missing = full clip (legacy projects load unchanged, malformed values are dropped on load, a new sound clears it). Logic in `src/audio/trim.ts`: kept length at least 50 ms, start < end, frame range from seconds and rate, 3 ms fades at cut edges only.
- Samples: the engine builds the buffer from the source cut to the track's trim (length equals the trim) and rebuilds when it changes; root detection is not re-run. Export applies the same cut (`src/song/render.ts`).
- Instruments: `applyTrackTrim` (`src/app/trimSound.ts`) analyses the cut source on release of a handle, then stores the new profile under the same sound id and swaps it in the engine; on failure the stored sound and trim are unchanged and the error shows under the waveform. A result for a sound the track no longer has is dropped (an instrument profile already written for it is deleted), and the profile and `trim` are flushed together. A finished draft chop is applied onto the current draft (`mergeTrimmed`) only when the draft still has the same source; other edits made meanwhile are kept. Requests during an analysis collapse to the latest (`latestRunner.ts`). Stored `pcm` of an instrument is the whole source; `instrument.pcm` is the cut one.
- UI: `TrimControl.svelte` inside `SampleControlsPanel` and `InstrumentControlsPanel`, used in the Generate draft (local until Use; Preview and Use wait for a running analysis) and in the Edit sound sheet. Undo history covers only the arrangement and is unchanged.

## CPU generation variant — 2026-10-12 (branch `cpu-variant`)

## Cloud generation and step limits — 2026-10-17 (branch `feature/stability-cloud`)

- What exists: an optional Cloud source for the Generate sheet. `src/gen/cloud.ts` posts multipart form data (prompt, `model=stable-audio-3`, duration, seed, steps, `output_format=wav`) to `https://api.stability.ai/v2beta/audio/stable-audio/text-to-audio` with `authorization: Bearer <key>` and `accept: audio/*`, polls `/v2beta/audio/results/{id}` every 2 s (202 = running, 200 = WAV) and gives up after 3 minutes. `src/gen/wav.ts` decodes the WAV; the result has the on-device `GenerationOutput` shape, so `prepareGeneration` is unchanged apart from an optional `cloud` flag that stores `meta.source: 'cloud'` and model `stable-audio-3`. Cost: a flat 8 credits per successful generation (Stability's OpenAPI spec).
- Limits: cloud steps 4..8 (default and maximum 8), length 1..12 s (the API minimum is 1 s), seed clamped to 0..4294967294. On-device steps now run 4..`MAX_STEPS` (64) in the sheet and in stored settings; default step values are unchanged (Sample 8, Instrument 16, cloud 8). Prompt wording for the cloud uses the Medium prompt builder.
- Key: `src/store/apiKey.ts` keeps it in localStorage `sa3daw.stabilityKey`; Models screen has the password field, Save, Clear and a masked state line; "Clear all app data" removes it. The key is redacted from error messages, details and log entries (tests feed it through every error path).
- Generate sheet: On device / Cloud switch persisted as `sa3daw.genSource`; Cloud is disabled without a key. The compat gate is unchanged: its hard failures (secure context, module workers, WebAssembly, Cache Storage, IndexedDB, Web Audio) still block the whole app.
